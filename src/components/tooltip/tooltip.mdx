### Accessibility

Tooltip uses `role="tooltip"` and `aria-describedby` to associate the tooltip content with its trigger. It appears on both hover and keyboard focus, and can be dismissed with the Escape key. Ensure trigger elements are focusable (buttons, links, or elements with `tabIndex={0}`).
